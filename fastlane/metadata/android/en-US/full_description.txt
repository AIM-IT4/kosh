<p><b>Kosh</b> (Sanskrit for <i>treasury</i>) is a private, offline-first money tracker built for how banking works in India. It reads your bank SMS, categorises every rupee and shows where you stand. No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers.</p>

<p><b>Automatic tracking</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Imports transactions from bank SMS as they arrive and from your inbox history: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union, IDFC FIRST, IndusInd, Yes, AU, Federal and DBS, plus a generic parser for other banks.</li>
<li>Smart categories from an open dataset of over 1,000 Indian merchants; it learns from every correction.</li>
<li>Transfers between your own accounts are recognised and never counted as spending. Income means what you earn; refunds and cashback come off spending.</li>
<li>Credit cards: outstanding, utilisation and statement bills from SMS, with due-date reminders.</li>
<li>Import bank statements (CSV), split transactions, tags, receipts and warranties.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Planning</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Left to spend until payday: your salary minus your savings target, what you've spent and what's still due.</li>
<li>Budgets with rollover and an envelope mode, and goals that show the monthly saving needed.</li>
<li>Subscriptions, EMIs and UPI AutoPay in one place, with reminders; loans with their schedule and tax benefit.</li>
<li>Money lent and borrowed, and an income tax estimate under both regimes.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Wealth and reports</b></p>
<ul>
<li>FD, EPF and NPS values update automatically; savings interest is estimated at your bank's rate.</li>
<li>A transparent net worth: every number shows where it came from.</li>
<li>PDF reports for any month or year, a year in review and a spending calendar.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy and security</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Everything stays on your phone. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher, its key held in the Android Keystore.</li>
<li>The offline edition has no internet permission at all. The standard edition only goes online to fetch public NPS prices.</li>
<li>App lock with fingerprint or face, privacy mode, screenshot blocking, and encrypted backups that can run automatically.</li>
<li>English and Hindi.</li>
</ul>

<p>Kosh is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later.</p>
