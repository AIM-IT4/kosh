## Kosh 2.1.0

**New**
- **Recurring & subscriptions:** repeats found in your spending (subscriptions, EMIs, UPI AutoPay / NACH mandates), what's due in the next 30 days, price-change alerts, and scheduled entries (rent, SIPs) that add themselves.
- **Loans & EMIs:** amortisation schedule, what's still owed (in net worth), prepayment calculator. Home-loan interest and principal flow into the tax planner (24(b), 80C).
- **Safe to spend** until payday on Home; budget **rollover** and **envelope** (zero-based) mode.
- **Tags**, **split transactions**, and **lent & borrowed**.
- **Receipts and warranties:** attach a bill photo or PDF (encrypted on the phone), with a reminder before a warranty ends.
- **Automatic backups:** daily or weekly encrypted backups to a folder you choose.
- **Bank statement CSV import**, **monthly/yearly PDF reports**, **year in review** and a **spending calendar**.
- **Hindi** (हिन्दी) option in More → Appearance.
- **More banks:** PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union Bank, IDFC FIRST, IndusInd, Yes Bank, AU and Federal Bank. 400+ more merchants recognised, and UPI AutoPay debits are no longer missed.

**Signing key (important)**
This build is signed with a new key (SHA-256 `cdd3eac48a0065202e9b78554d6d2e996547be05358a911941c1ea21ba9f560e`).

your data, will replace these files. Until then, only install this on a phone without Kosh.

`Kosh.apk` is the standard edition; `Kosh-offline.apk` has no internet permission at all. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
