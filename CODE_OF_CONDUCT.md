# Code of conduct

Kosh follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and sharing anyone's private information (including financial data) are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **jha.8@alumni.iitj.ac.in**. Reports are handled confidentially.
